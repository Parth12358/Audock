# Embeddings

Embeddings map text to vectors so that **semantic similarity becomes geometric distance**.
Nearby vectors mean related meaning. This note is the worked example that ships with the
vault — a distilled [[20261015-machine-learning|Machine Learning]] concept backed by a verbatim
