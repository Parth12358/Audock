# Maps of Content

A Map of Content (MOC) is a hand-curated hub that combines tag-like grouping, folder-like proximity, and deliberate ordering. Nick Milo's advice: create a MOC at a 'mental squeeze point', not up front. MOCs are the entry points into a graph of [[atomic-notes]] and [[evergreen-notes]]; Audock keeps them in `maps/` and links them from the index.
