# Evergreen notes

Andy Matuschak's evergreen notes are written to accumulate over time: atomic (see [[atomic-notes]]), concept-oriented, and **densely linked** — the web of links, not folders, is the retrieval structure. Titling a note as a claim makes it easy to link and to supersede when it changes (see [[epistemic-status]]).
