# Epistemic status

Digital gardens mark each note seedling → budding → evergreen so readers know what they are looking at; marking status is what makes it safe to publish something imperfect. Audock maps this to `status: draft/stable/superseded` and prefers explicit supersede over silent decay — see [[markdown-as-source-of-truth]] and [[dedup-gate]].
